\section{Locked model and blend notes}
\label{app:C}

Appendix~C explains what the locked train-2 path is and what it is not.

The locked path loads the \texttt{saved\_model} export and applies the blend in Eq.~\eqref{eq:blend}. In hazard order solar, radiation, temperature, moonquakes, micrometeorites, dust, the nonzero machine-learning weights are solar activity~$0.4$ and radiation~$0.05$. Temperature, moonquakes, micrometeorites, and dust stay on physics or proxy paths under this lock.

This lock is the accuracy baseline for Windows~A and~B. It is not the client V1 prototype stack. It is not an equal-weight ensemble. Do not point study runners at \texttt{saved\_model\_train2\_locked} for the paper tables; that folder uses different weights. Skill charts drawn from this lock must use within-hazard scales only.

Readers who need the exact loader entry point should follow Appendix~E under Window~A and Window~B.

\section{Reproduction steps by study}
\label{app:E}

This appendix tells you what each study is for, where to go in the package, and what to run so results match or closely match the paper. Use model directory \texttt{saved\_model} and blend $[0.4,0.05,0,0,0,0]$. Do not mix client V1 prototype outputs into locked tables.

\subsection*{Window A (night-dominant locked study)}

Purpose: locked 14-day skill and RMSE at equatorial, Apollo~11, and Nobile Rim sites (night-dominant window).

Where to go: \texttt{studies/} and \texttt{logs/study\_14day\_window\_A\_night/}.

What to run:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.2em]
\item Open \texttt{studies/README.md}.
\item Run:
\begin{verbatim}
python scripts/run_14day_flagship_study.py \
  --start 2026-07-05 --end 2026-07-18 --window-label A \
  --out-dir logs/study_14day_window_A_night \
  --model-dir saved_model \
  --csv data/ingested_extended/extended_training_window_B_ready.csv
\end{verbatim}
\item Open \texttt{logs/study\_14day\_window\_A\_night/summary.json}.
\item Compare solar and radiation RMSE to \texttt{reported\_window\_rmse.md} and Fig.~\ref{fig:wa}.
\end{enumerate}

Expected: solar about 4.10 versus replica 4.66; radiation about 0.119 versus 0.102; physics ties on temperature, moonquakes, and dust; micrometeorite hybrid RMSE 0.0 (circular).

\subsection*{Sanity check before claiming reproduction}

After any locked run, re-open \texttt{papers/appendix\_numerical\_tables/reported\_window\_rmse.md} and confirm solar and radiation RMSE match the paper within normal floating-point tolerance. If they diverge, check that \texttt{--model-dir} is \texttt{saved\_model} and that blend weights print as $[0.4,0.05,0,0,0,0]$.
