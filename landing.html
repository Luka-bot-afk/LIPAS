Why L.I.P.A.S.

Lunar surface work needs hazard forecasts that stay physically honest.

The Moon is hostile by default - no atmosphere, no magnetic shield, extreme day/night temperatures, and continuous micrometeoroid flux. Solar storms can spike radiation with little warning for EVA crews.

L.I.P.A.S. closes that gap by blending NOAA/SWPC-driven machine learning with deterministic thermal physics, then exposing the result on maps, a 3D globe, and an EVA mission planner you can actually act on.
