Data sources

Public NASA and NOAA space-weather archives, lunar mission datasets, and the OMNI heliophysics stack used by lunar LIPAS.

NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center

Kp index, solar flux, GOES X-rays, and proton flux. swpc.noaa.gov

NASA OMNI / heliospheric archives

Solar wind and geomagnetic context used in training and live driver features.

JPL Horizons

Daily Mercury heliocentric vectors (r_AU). ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons

MESSENGER magnetosphere (literature)

Dipole ~195 nT equatorial surface field and mean magnetopause ~1.45 R_M (Anderson et al. 2011; Winslow et al. 2013). Used as the analytic magnetosphere, not a re-analysis of MAG files.

NASA LRO / Trek WMS

Not used on the Mercury dashboard. Lunar globe tiles remain in the repo for the original Moon stack.

NASA DONKI

Space weather event catalog for flare/CME context in ingestion pipelines.

Diviner, Apollo, LDEX climatology

Thermal and dust context from published lunar mission datasets used in the original Moon model. Mercury temperature is radiative + nightside floor, not Diviner.

USGS / lunar site catalogs

Six Hermean sites (Caloris, Rachmaninoff, equator, Beethoven, Prokofiev, Chao Meng-Fu) with USGS/MESSENGER-style coordinates.

LIPAS combines public archives with physics-constrained forecasts for lunar sites. The Mercury layer reuses the same 1 AU drivers with magnetosphere physics and does not apply the lunar LSTM weights.
